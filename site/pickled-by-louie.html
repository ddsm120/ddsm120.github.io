My inspiration

My friends grow cucumbers in their veggie garden and pickle the extra harvest. After receiving jars of homemade pickles, I wanted to create a fun, cheeky logo and stickers to spruce up her gifts, with a playful pickle that doesn’t take itself too seriously.

The visual idea

The wordmark gives the jar a bold focal point, while the standalone pickle icon offers a smaller brand signature. Colour variations explore how the same identity can feel consistent across contrasting backgrounds.
