My inspiration

I created Soju Buddy out of my love for Korean BBQ and the culture of sharing food and drinks with friends.

The visual idea

Bold typography gives the identity a consistent voice, while the illustrated characters bring variety to the bottle range. The same characters extend onto pins, carrying the personality beyond the packaging.
