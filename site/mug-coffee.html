My inspiration

Coffee is part of my morning routine, a small ritual that helps me start the day. Seeing creative coffee packaging inspired me to design a brand of my own.

The visual idea

The roast indicators use both words and filled circles to distinguish light, medium and dark coffee. Consistent label layouts hold the range together, with colour adding another point of difference between packs.
