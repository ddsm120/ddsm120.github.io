Selected work / 03 / Branding

Anchorage Cafe branding

I created the brand identity, signage and collateral for Anchorage Cafe, a new shipping container cafe serving guests arriving at the wharf and waiting for their ferry. The project connected a new food and coffee offering with the wider resort experience, while giving cafe staff practical tools to maintain menus and food labels.

Project overview

Tangalooma Island Resort Wharf · Brand identity, wayfinding and launch collateral

Explore more work ↗

The brief

The wharf previously had only a drinks vending machine. The new cafe introduced coffee and snack food, including pies, cookies and sandwiches. My brief was to give the cafe its own identity and prepare the signage and collateral needed for opening.

My role and approach

I visited the site to understand the guest journey from arrival at the wharf to ferry departure, alongside the container’s structure, orientation and setup. This informed how the branding and signage would work in the space.

I worked closely with the wharf manager to coordinate the design requirements and ensure the operational materials were ready ahead of opening day.

What I delivered

  • A visual identity that used colours similar to the resort’s existing brand, giving the cafe its own personality while maintaining a clear connection to Tangalooma.
  • Wayfinding signage designed and positioned at key points along the arrival route to guide guests towards the cafe.
  • Menu and food-label templates that cafe staff could update independently.
  • Branded collateral and signage ready for launch.

The outcome

Anchorage Cafe opened with its brand identity, signage and guest-facing materials in place. The project brought together branding, the physical guest journey and the everyday needs of the staff running the cafe.
