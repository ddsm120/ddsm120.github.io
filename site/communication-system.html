Selected work / 04 / Signage & Guest Experience

Tours & Activities communication system

I developed a permanent information display for the Tours & Activities area, replacing loose photocopied flyers with branded panels guests could browse independently. The display made tour information available outside desk opening hours and was later adopted for a second installation at the wharf.

Project overview

Tangalooma Island Resort · Guest information, display design and wayfinding

Explore more work ↗

The challenge

Staff created flyers to answer common questions and promote tours in a busy guest area. Printed in greyscale on lightweight paper, the flyers folded over in their stands, looked inconsistent with the resort’s brand and required regular printing and restocking.

The brief was to redesign the information and find a better display solution. Reducing paper waste also supported the resort’s environmental priorities.

My role and approach

I visited the Tours & Activities area to familiarise myself with the space and discuss the team’s day-to-day challenges, particularly during peak times. I observed how guests moved through the area, what they asked staff, and whether they picked up the information presented. I also consulted with other departments and concessionaires to determine the necessary information and whether the proposed approach would work in practice.

What I delivered

Inspired by real estate window displays, I developed a fixed panel system mounted on the glass beside the tours desk. Guests could view it from both sides and browse information without waiting for a staff member.

  • Branded colour panels presenting key tour and activity information, including opening hours.
  • QR codes linking to the resort website for further booking details.
  • Imagery and layouts consistent with the website, connecting the physical display with the online experience.
  • A permanent display that remained available outside desk opening hours and removed the need to print and restock loose flyers each day.

The outcome

The display replaced the daily flyer routine with a consistent presentation of guest information. The colour panels were clearly arranged and visible from a distance, making information easier to browse independently.

Following its use at the resort, a second installation was implemented at the wharf.
