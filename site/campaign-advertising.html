Campaign & advertising

As part of my role at Tangalooma, I developed creative work across multiple campaigns, supporting seasonal sales objectives and promoting resort experiences during key travel periods.

Project overview

Tangalooma Island Resort · Visual direction and artwork across print, digital and outdoor channels

Explore more work ↗

Creative approach

Following an initial marketing brainstorm and a loose brief, I developed the campaign’s visual direction. This included selecting hero imagery, developing the tagline treatment, choosing typography and establishing the overall look and feel while maintaining consistency with the resort’s brand.

I prepared concepts and mockups for discussion and feedback. Once approved, I produced the final artwork and adapted it across the required channels and formats.

My role and deliverables

  • Campaign concepts, mockups and visual direction.
  • Billboards and outdoor advertising.
  • Print advertising and brochures.
  • Website and social media assets.
  • Artwork for paid digital advertising.
  • Supplier liaison as part of artwork production and delivery.

Selected examples

These examples show work across day-cruise, whale-watching, resort sale and event promotions, including social advertising, a day-trip brochure, installed billboards, bus advertising and guest-facing signage.
